# Competitor and positioning research

Internal working document. **Do not publish.** Nothing here is Seudocom information, and nothing on the public site was copied from these companies.

Reviewed from each company's public website unless noted. All claims are the companies' own and were not independently verified.

## Direct comparison

## Additional firms

These came from search results and were not reviewed in depth. Search surfaced staffing firms rather than further construction contractors.

- **TekCom Resources:** telecom staffing across OSP, permitting, construction and program management.
- **MercuryZ:** workforce organized by deployment phase, for carriers, ISPs and utilities.
- **PeopleReady / TrueBlue:** on-demand fiber crews.
- **Broadstaff:** wireless and wireline staffing.

## What this means for Seudocom

**Worth adopting (as patterns, not copy)**
- Navigation that serves two audiences: clients and job seekers.
- An inquiry CTA that is always in the header.
- Published hiring steps.
- An application on the site itself, rather than an external job board.
- EEO and accommodation wording, and a separate subcontractor route, if the client wants them.

**Where Seudocom can differ**
- **Identity:** competitors cluster around white, blue and navy corporate looks. Black and lime brutalism is immediately distinct.
- **Credibility without track record:** competitors lean on years, named carriers and awards, which Seudocom can't honestly match. Plain language and transparency are the alternative.
- **One partner:** few competitors lead with build, maintain and staff as a single simple offer.

**Not worth competing on yet:** capability lists, market counts, safety claims, carrier names. Add them only when they are true and confirmed.

## Recommended structure

Home, Network Services, Workforce Solutions, About, Careers, Contact. Built as specified in the brief, plus Image Credits (required by the photo licenses) and a 404 page.
